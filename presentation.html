The Problem

  • Traditional rule-based AML generates 90 false positives per 1,700 transactions.
  • Compliance officers are overwhelmed chasing false alerts, wasting operational hours.
  • Complex laundering (structuring, smurfing, layering) evades simple threshold rules.

Agentic Solution & Architecture

Our system maps exactly to the 5 mandated hackathon requirements:

1. EDA Tool
Baseline profiling, distributions, and missing value visualization.
2. Feature Engineering Tool
Rolling sums, velocity metrics, and rapid cashout flags.
3. Anomaly Detection Tool
4-Layer Stack: Compliance Rules + Z-Score + Random Forest + Graph Network.
4. Risk Classification Tool
Weighted Risk Score mapped to Low, Medium, or High risk tiers.
5. Explanation Component
SHAP local explanations + Auto-SAR narrative generator + Escalation Action (Monitor/Report).

Dynamic Agentic Behavior

Unlike standard ML, our Orchestrator builds a dynamic execution plan based on intent.

"Find structuring in last 30 days"
→ Applies time filter & rule engine only. Skips full EDA and ML pipeline.
"Is ACC4521 suspicious?"
→ Single-entity lookup + SHAP explanation + on-demand Risk Classification.
Provided Outputs per Query:
  • Query-aware execution summary
  • Top suspicious transactions
  • Risk level & Escalation Action
  • Flag Reason (Explainability)
